What is Mashrou3i Publisher?

Mashrou3i Publisher is a small internal tool used by one teacher, Prof. Fellah Mohamed (Makkawi Mohamed Secondary School, Algeria), to publish the lesson videos he produces himself to his own YouTube channel, «مشروعي · علوم الطبيعة والحياة».

How it works

  1. The teacher adds a finished lesson video (video.mp4) and its lesson card (title, level, unit, objectives) to his private lesson repository on GitHub.
  2. A GitHub Actions workflow builds the video's title, description, tags and playlist from that lesson card.
  3. The workflow uploads the video to the teacher's own YouTube channel using the YouTube Data API, sets its thumbnail and adds it to the matching playlist.
  4. The resulting YouTube video ID is saved back to the repository so the «مشروعي» classroom app can play the video through the standard YouTube embedded player.

Who uses it

Only the channel owner. The tool has no public sign-up, no other users and does not access any other YouTube channel or any viewer data.
